About Nural Islam

Everest School Islamic Jamaa

Our Mission & Vision

Nural Islam (The Light of Islam) is the official Islamic Jamaa of Everest School, located in the Alamgana district of the Oromia region, Ethiopia.

We are dedicated to fostering a strong community built on faith, mutual respect, and the pursuit of knowledge. We organize daily prayers, weekly halaqas, and community service events to help our students grow spiritually and socially.

Our vision is to be a beacon of light for the next generation, guiding them to excel in both their academic studies and their spiritual journey.
